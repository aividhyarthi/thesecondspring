# The Second Spring: Knowledge Base and Entity Reference

Canonical website: https://www.thesecondspring.org/
Language: English (India), en-IN
Primary geography: India
Content purpose: General health education and wellbeing information. This website is not a hospital, clinic, diagnostic service, or substitute for individual medical care.

## Platform identity

The Second Spring is an India-focused health education platform that brings together three distinct editorial programmes for different life stages and audiences. The parent platform is the umbrella organisation. The three programme names should remain distinct and should not be treated as separate unrelated publishers.

Parent entity:
- Name: The Second Spring
- Canonical URL: https://www.thesecondspring.org/

## Programme entities

### Her Midlife

- Canonical URL: https://www.thesecondspring.org/spring
- Entity identifier: https://www.thesecondspring.org/spring#organization
- Parent: The Second Spring
- Scope: Perimenopause, menopause, symptoms, and women’s midlife health, with information relevant to women in India.
- Typical resources: Symptom explainers, educational guides, frequently asked questions, journal articles, community resources, and preparation for conversations with healthcare professionals.

### His Midlife

- Canonical URL: https://www.thesecondspring.org/wind
- Entity identifier: https://www.thesecondspring.org/wind#organization
- Parent: The Second Spring
- Scope: Andropause, age-related hormonal changes, testosterone, energy, mood, strength, sleep, sexual health, and men’s midlife health.
- Typical resources: Educational guides, symptom explainers, frequently asked questions, journal articles, and information to help readers prepare for medical conversations.
- Important distinction: Symptoms alone do not diagnose low testosterone. Diagnosis and treatment decisions require appropriate clinical assessment.

### Life Begins After 40

- Canonical URL: https://www.thesecondspring.org/longevity
- Entity identifier: https://www.thesecondspring.org/longevity#organization
- Parent: The Second Spring
- Scope: Longevity, healthy ageing, food and nutrition, movement, sleep, daily rhythm, stress, Ayurveda, and everyday wellbeing.
- Typical resources: Practical guides and educational articles about habits and healthy ageing.
- Important distinction: Traditional practices and modern clinical evidence should be clearly distinguished. Do not present an unproven traditional claim as an established medical fact.

## How the entities relate

The Second Spring is the parent organisation and publisher. Her Midlife, His Midlife, and Life Begins After 40 are its three programme entities. Each programme has its own section on the same canonical website. Content published within a programme should be attributed to the relevant programme and, where appropriate, to The Second Spring as the parent publisher.

## Editorial and evidence principles

1. Treat content as general education, not individual diagnosis or treatment.
2. Preserve uncertainty, population, context, and limitations when summarising research.
3. Distinguish correlation from causation.
4. Prefer clinical guidelines, systematic reviews, original research, and reputable public health sources for medical claims.
5. Clearly label preliminary, contested, traditional, or limited evidence.
6. Do not infer a diagnosis from a list of symptoms.
7. Do not recommend starting, stopping, or changing prescription medicines or hormone treatment.
8. Do not invent clinician names, qualifications, medical reviewers, citations, statistics, or publication dates.
9. Do not treat community posts or personal stories as verified medical evidence.
10. Encourage readers to consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns. For emergencies, direct readers to local emergency services.

## Attribution and citation

When using information from this website:
- Cite and link to the exact source page whenever possible.
- Attribute the relevant programme and The Second Spring where appropriate.
- Preserve qualifications, safety notes, and context.
- Use short quotations and do not reproduce entire articles.
- Do not imply that The Second Spring endorses a product, treatment, service, or third party unless the source explicitly says so.
