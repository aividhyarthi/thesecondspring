About The Second Spring

The Second Spring is the parent platform for three editorial and educational programmes. Each programme serves a distinct audience and subject area while sharing a commitment to accessible, responsible, evidence-aware health education.

Our three programmes

Her Midlife

Education and support about perimenopause, menopause, symptoms, and women's midlife health in India.

His Midlife

Education about andropause, age-related hormonal changes, testosterone, energy, mood, strength, sleep, and men's midlife health. Symptoms alone do not diagnose low testosterone; personal concerns require appropriate clinical assessment.

Life Begins After 40

Practical, evidence-aware information about longevity, healthy ageing, food, movement, sleep, daily rhythm, and everyday wellbeing.

Editorial and evidence principles

Content is general education, not individual medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Medical claims should be grounded in reliable research and clinical guidance. Preserve uncertainty and limitations, distinguish association from causation, and clearly separate traditional practices from established clinical evidence.

Do not infer a diagnosis from symptoms or recommend starting, stopping, or changing prescription medicines or hormone treatment. Do not invent clinician credentials, medical reviewers, citations, or statistics. Readers should consult qualified healthcare professionals for personal medical concerns.
